About me

I am a PhD student at the Shanghai Center for Mathematical Sciences, Fudan University, advised by Xiaolei Wu.

My research is in geometric group theory and topological dynamics, with a focus on Thompson groups and their generalizations, ample groupoids, and topological full groups.

I expect to complete my PhD in 2027. Before coming to Fudan, I received my B.S. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from Sichuan University in 2022.

Research interests

  • Geometric group theory. Thompson groups and their generalizations; embedding problems for groups; finiteness properties; group cohomology and bounded cohomology; Dehn functions; amenability; Hopficity.
  • Groupoids and dynamics. Ample groupoids, topological full groups, homology, and K-theory.
  • Geometric topology. L2-invariants, BNSR invariants, hyperbolic geometry, and manifolds fibering over the circle.
